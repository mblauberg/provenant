# Assumptions and owner-away steering

Make decisions only within the user's current authority envelope. A session
checkpoint may record an unconfirmed assumption, its basis, reversal cost,
evidence references, and the owner decision needed. It is not a separate
ledger or an authority source.

- Keep the newest user direction linked from the mission goal and follow it.
  Reopen the owner direction after compaction or a new session.
- Continue independent work that is within scope when one task waits on a user
  gate. Do not widen scope to fill an idle frontier.
- Make reversible, in-authority choices when the approved scope allows them.
  Park contested choices and one-way doors for the named owner; include the
  question, evidence, and what it blocks.
- A gate stops only the affected task. Continue other authorised work that
  does not depend on the answer.
- When the owner returns, provide a concise list of what landed, what remains,
  which decisions were made within authority, and which decisions need the
  owner's answer.
- Once an assumption is confirmed, put any durable decision in its project
  owner. Keep live assignments in Fabric tasks and run lanes.
