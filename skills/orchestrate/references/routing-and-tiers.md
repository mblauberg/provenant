# Routing, councils and degradation

Inspect `provenant route list` and resolve a proposed pool with
`provenant route resolve --route POOL`. Catalogue presence does not establish
live availability. The [model-routing runbook](../../../docs/runbooks/model-routing.md)
owns pool resolution, pacing and state locations.

For preset runs, tune declared parameters, stages or roles. Top-level
`--route`, `--model` and `--effort` conflict with `--preset`.
For a direct bounded task, an explicit route bypasses automatic preset
selection. Choose that deliberately rather than adding `--route senior`
to every submission.

## Second family or council

Use a clean-context reviewer to reduce anchoring. Give it the approved spec,
base and immutable subject; keep the author's diagnosis out. Use a second
family when the HARNESS review ladder requires it or when a weak oracle
justifies an additional independent view. Different model names alone do not
prove different answering families.

`review-council` frames a `subject`, runs `lenses`, then `verify` and
`synthesis`. The verifier tries to falsify findings; the synthesis settles
them against evidence. `code-review` owns findings, severities and dispositions.
For a design question without a code subject, use independent branches in
`fan-out-synthesise` and a chair decision against the stated criteria.

In a tiered council, cheaper scouts can gather claims; stronger judges test
them and a chair resolves what remains. A cheap finding is useful evidence
without satisfying a required review leg. Count review independence and tier
against HARNESS, including its same-family replacement when a required family
is unavailable. A majority vote cannot overrule a failing criterion.

Paired-primary work still has one chair and one acting owner per stage.
Record stage, base revision, scope, expected output, peer and reserved gates
in the durable assignment. Rotate the acting owner explicitly; concurrent
ownership of a coupled write is not pairing.

## Inspect actual separation

Graph roles can request `family = "cross_preferred"` or
`family = "cross_required"`. Prefer permits a recorded substitution; require
can make a route unavailable. Use the separation record and resolved
`adapter/model@effort` from the attempt rather than inferring assurance from
a requested pool. Record author, reviewer, lens and unavailable legs.

A missing `verifier` route can substitute `senior`; a missing `web` route
can substitute `junior`. Neither substitution proves the requested family or
external research capability. Confirm the evidence actually obtained.
Replace an unavailable required review leg with an independent reviewer
through a different lens as HARNESS requires; report any remaining gap.

Keep unsupported effort values out of requests. Prefer per-run controls;
changing global provider configuration requires authority for that path and
action. Resource pressure calls for fewer lanes or narrower work, never
credential access or a weaker authority boundary.

## Choose evidence over consensus

A compiler, targeted test or differential run can settle a concrete property
more cheaply than a panel. Use councils for the remaining judgement, state the
criteria up front, and preserve dissent that identifies an unverified criterion.
Scale by independent questions rather than repeating the same prompt across
more agents. A second family adds coverage only when its result is independently
grounded and its actual route meets the required assurance.
