# Emotional design

Critique the rendered surface at Norman's three levels: visceral, what it feels
like at first sight; behavioural, what using the flow feels like; and
reflective, what the user remembers and makes of it afterwards. Use them as
lenses on the experience, not a scoring formula, and ask what each level says
about the user's goal.

## Moments

Peak-end effects have some support in product-use research, but the evidence
is correlational and recalled, not a design law. Never use it as licence to
manipulate attention or spend. Give one moment deliberate care, usually task
completion, rather than decorating every step.

For a flow, name:

- **First impression:** what the user should feel on arrival.
- **Doing the task:** what using it should feel like.
- **Takeaway:** what they should remember.
- **The moment:** the one completion or milestone that gets care.

Afterwards, check the moment reads as earned by the work done and proportionate
to the action, not decorative. The more often a moment recurs, the quieter it
should be; set intensity with the frequency guidance in [motion](motion.md).

## Never gamify

The user's own goal comes before any engagement metric.

- Honest private progress, such as a progress ring or completion marker, is not
  a coercive mechanic and needs no opt-out.
- Optional engagement notifications, sharing, and social or competitive
  mechanics are opt-in, and comparative mechanics such as leaderboards and
  rankings default to private. Task-critical alerts the user relies on, such as
  security, deadlines, or failures, are not engagement mechanics.
- No false urgency, fear-of-missing-out framing, or variable or random rewards
  to drive activity.
- No loss-aversion or shame copy, and no confirmshaming: declining is never
  framed as a loss.
- Cancelling, downgrading, or deleting an account adds no friction beyond
  signing up, except required identity, security, or legal verification.
- A streak-like mechanic tracks personal consistency only, and allows grace
  before a lapse counts against it.

When a request asks for a mechanic this list rules out, reshape it to the list
and say what changed; do not build it verbatim or refuse the whole feature.
