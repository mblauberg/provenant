# Content and conversion

Organise content around the user's next decision. Establish information
hierarchy, terminology, primary and secondary actions, progressive disclosure,
and the path from arrival to first value. Labels describe outcomes; help and
onboarding appear near the moment of need without replacing discoverable
controls.

For narrative and landing surfaces, infer audience, offer, traffic context,
primary action, objections, and available proof from the brief and project.
Choose the narrative archetype from that intent, not a stock section template.
Align the arrival message with its traffic source; above the fold, state the
audience and outcome in the headline and make one primary CTA clear.

Build a proof inventory before drafting trust claims. Lead with benefits backed
by available evidence, place proof beside its claim, and address objections
through the most suitable mechanism, such as comparison, FAQ, guarantee, or
risk reversal. Preserve continuity between promise, CTA, and destination. Make
an explicit metadata decision for title/description, canonical and social
cards, structured or answer-oriented content, and SEO indexing versus noindex.
Do not invent customers, metrics, guarantees, urgency, or legal claims.

Before coding a landing surface, produce a compact pre-code handoff when it
will reduce rework: audience and promise; chosen section order; hero copy and
primary CTA; benefit and how-it-works scaffolds; proof and objection placement;
the chosen layout rationale; final CTA continuity; and the metadata/SEO
decision. Build section by section from that argument arc. When the request
already settles these choices, record them and proceed without an approval
round-trip.

Empty and first-run states should orient, explain value, and offer an honest
next action. Loading, error, permission, offline, and destructive states need
specific, actionable content consistent with the interaction contract. Tone
follows seriousness and recovery; novelty must not hide meaning. Accessible
names and alternative text describe purpose rather than visual jokes or
keywords.

This owner sets UI content hierarchy and constraints. Route substantial copy
rewriting to `engineering-writing` or `natural-writing`, and preserve approved
product, legal, localisation, and brand owners.
