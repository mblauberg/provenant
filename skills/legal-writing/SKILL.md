# Australian legal writing

Drafting assistance, not legal advice. Verify current law, forms and procedure
against official sources, and keep qualified review for anything filing-facing.
Sending, signing, filing, serving and lodging stay with the user; drafting
authorises none of them. A supplied form, forum rules and stricter project
instructions override this skill's defaults.

Use it for Australian legal instruments, filing-facing text and legal
correspondence. Subject matter alone is not the trigger: an article or
plain-language explanation about law goes to `writing`.

## Approach

1. Record the forum adapter before drafting: forum, stage, document function,
   mandatory form, labels, deadlines. If it is incomplete, produce only a
   marked internal draft. See [instruments](references/instruments.md).
2. Classify each paragraph by the Legal Function Test and move wrong-home
   material before polishing. See [core](references/core.md).
3. Lock legal status, source anchors, exact quotations, authorities, labels,
   offer terms, non-admission and non-waiver wording, deadlines, attachments and
   redactions.
4. Draft the minimum complete text: relief or answer first, one proposition per
   paragraph, exact pinpoints, no internal agent language.
5. Lint with `provenant check writing PATHS --rules legal`, then check
   what the lint cannot see.

## Fixed points

- Never invent or silently alter an authority, instrument, finding, rule title,
  forum term, decision-maker label, source anchor or quotation. Flag a
  suspected error; do not correct the record.
- A protective, police-issued or interim safety instrument stays a dated source
  at the status its verified text supports. It is not a finding.
- Never weaken a threshold, drop an anchor or change forum wording to make text
  read more naturally.
- Apply `writing` for shared house style.
