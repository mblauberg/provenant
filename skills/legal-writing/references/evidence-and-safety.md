# Evidence, verification and safety

## Source boundary

Follow the project's source-routing and evidence protocol. An index, manifest,
OCR layer, machine transcript, render, generated summary, working note or
filename locates a source; it never proves a filing fact. Open the underlying
source before relying on it.

- Every factual assertion in filing-facing text carries the most exact anchor
  available: document title and date, page, paragraph, exhibit or annexure label,
  transcript page and line, message timestamp. Do not cut an anchor to shorten a
  sentence; shorten around it. A fact with no anchor is anchored, qualified as
  instruction or argument, or removed.
- Audio and video: cite the authoritative recording and a checked timestamp. A
  machine or party-prepared transcript is a locator until verified; never call it
  official or certified unless it is.
- Another proceeding's filed material proves procedural status, notice, a party's
  stated position or an inconsistency. It does not prove the facts asserted in it.
  Cite the canonical source or a fresh affidavit annexing it.
- Chat exports: a `.txt` export is a locator. For filing, use a readable PDF or
  screenshot, a filed annexure page or a targeted redacted extract, with visible
  timestamp, participant and label.
- An email thread proves transmission, notice, receipt or timing. An accusation
  in it is not a fact.

## Claim status

Preserve the status of each proposition when rewriting. Verb choice carries it.

| Status | Wording |
|---|---|
| Source-proved fact | 'records', 'states', 'shows' |
| Sworn fact | 'I say', 'I observed', 'I received' |
| Allegation or contention | 'alleges', 'asserts', 'submits' |
| Admission | 'admits', 'accepts', 'does not dispute' |
| Disputed fact | 'disputes', 'does not admit', 'has been unable to reconcile' |
| Procedural fact | 'was filed', 'was served', 'was listed', 'was stayed' |
| Inference | 'supports', 'is consistent with' |
| Source limit | 'does not establish', 'proves notice but not truth' |

An email accusation, another proceeding's submission, a safety document, an
internal note or an unresolved issue never becomes established fact, and a
contention never becomes affidavit evidence. Report `filed`, `served`, `lodged`,
`sealed`, `signed`, `witnessed` or `sent` only with evidence of that state.

## Authorities and quotations

Fabricated authorities, invented instruments and silently altered rule titles
are the recurring drafting-agent failures.

- Use only an authority already on the matter's verified list, or one you opened
  and pinpoint-checked against a primary source this session. Otherwise leave it
  out and record the gap.
- State each holding at its fact-specific altitude. Do not inflate a narrow ruling.
- Never add an instrument, notice, order term or finding the record does not
  contain, however plausible.
- Preserve rule titles, statute names and court or decision-maker labels exactly.
  If one looks wrong or obsolete, flag it.
- Before external use of an AI-assisted draft, check the forum's current AI,
  authorship and certification requirements. A human opens every authority and
  quotation the forum requires checked; AI does not attest its own authorities.
  Nothing confidential, suppressed or privileged goes to a tool whose disclosure
  boundary does not permit it.

## Verification record

For each rule, form, procedural statement or important fact, keep: the
proposition, source and issuing body, version or effective date, exact pinpoint,
whether the source itself was opened, and any conflict. Check commencement,
amendment, transitional provisions and the relevant date before calling a source
current. Keep competing figures and attribute each.

## Stop and escalate

Stop at a marked internal draft when: forum, stage or form is uncertain; official sources
conflict; the wording would turn a contention into a finding; confidentiality,
privilege, suppression, safety or redaction is unresolved; an authority or quote
cannot be pinpoint-verified; or a signature, certification, undertaking,
concession, filing, service or communication lacks current user authority.
Record the competing readings and their consequence; do not hide the gap in
polished prose.

Each external act and recipient (file, lodge, serve, upload, sign, witness,
certify, disclose, send) is a separate user-authorised step.

## Safety-sensitive material

- Name each source at its true status: application, allegation, police-issued
  instrument, interim measure, final order, and reasons containing a finding are
  different things. A protective instrument proves that it exists and what it
  says, not every underlying allegation. Use finding language only where an
  authorised source contains the finding.
- Do not call a person a perpetrator, diagnose behaviour, or assert coercive
  control or criminal conduct unless the source, current law,
  document function and authorised position support that exact claim.
- State why the material matters in this document and use the least sensitive
  material that proves the point. Keep safety context separate from merits proof.
  Keep graphic, medical, reproductive-health, child and third-party detail out
  unless necessary, supported and authorised.
- Before external use, review for: residential and service addresses, unsafe
  contact or location data, employer, school or routine detail, account and
  health identifiers, children and third parties, metadata, headers and
  attachment properties, and broad message histories where a targeted extract
  suffices.
- Never assume redaction, confidential filing, restricted access or alternative
  service is permitted: verify the mechanism, what each side and the
  decision-maker receive, and whether an unredacted copy is required. Redact the
  source and every derivative layer, then inspect the render for copyable or
  recoverable text.
- Do not disclose protected contact details, waive a protection, publish
  sensitive material or contact a prohibited recipient without current authority.
