# AGLC4

The Guide controls; check against the published edition rather than recalling
patterns from another style. Pinpoint every attributed proposition.

## Where it applies

Academic articles, theses and chapters; advices and research memoranda where the
recipient expects it. Filed submissions, affidavits and orders follow the
forum's own practice, which is usually not AGLC. Correspondence uses plain
identification (title, date, pinpoint). Do not convert a forum's citation
practice into AGLC for tidiness, or AGLC into loose prose citation while
condensing.

## Slips to check

- Round and square brackets around the year swapped: ask whether the year or the
  volume identifies the volume.
- Report series abbreviation guessed: verify in the Guide and the report.
- Medium neutral citation used where an authorised report exists.
- Case name not italicised, or `v` given a full stop. Statute without its
  bracketed jurisdiction.
- Missing pinpoint: add the page or paragraph, or drop the attribution.
- Short forms and `Ibid` pointing at a renumbered footnote: re-check after any cut,
  insertion or reordering.
- Quotation altered to match house style: the source's spelling, capitalisation
  and punctuation stand. Mark `[sic]`, omissions and added emphasis.
- Citation copied from a secondary source: verify against the primary source.

Formatting does not verify. A neatly formatted fabrication is worse than an untidy
real citation; the authority rules in
[evidence and safety](evidence-and-safety.md) still apply.
