# Instruments

## Forum adapter

Before drafting, record the minimum verified adapter: jurisdiction, forum,
proceeding and stage; document, function, reader and the relief it supports;
current party, decision-maker and document labels; mandatory form, fields,
annexures and signing; current length, layout, citation, filing, service and
deadline rules; evidence-use, confidentiality, suppression and redaction rules;
official source, version and date checked; open points for qualified review.

Do not infer one forum's terms or procedure from another, and do not treat house
style as cross-forum compliance. If the adapter is incomplete, draft only a
marked internal version. Precedence: governing law, orders and official forum
material; the supplied form or filing specification; project and matter
instructions; the adapter; this skill's defaults. Never silently repair a
conflict: preserve the source wording and flag it.

## What goes where

| Document | Keep | Move out |
|---|---|---|
| Form-bound application | Exact fields, verified labels, concise grounds | Narrative beyond the field, legal conclusions |
| Affidavit, witness statement | First-person facts, linked documents | Argument, characterisation, reasons for relief |
| Submissions | Issues, rules, argument, relief | New evidence, unsupported fact |
| Proposed orders | Actor, act, object, deadline or trigger | Reasons, evidence recitals, advocacy |
| Chronology | Date, event, source, relevance | Free-standing argument |
| Exhibit or annexure index | Exact labels, titles, dates, pages | Analysis |
| Internal advice | Assumptions, competing readings, gaps | Anything sent externally unreviewed |

## Orders

An order is a command, not a submission. Check that the forum can make it and
the applicant has a route; it does not duplicate another order, a form field or
an automatic consequence; it does not ask one forum to control another; any
condition, undertaking or recital is classified correctly; and numbering and
cross-references survive the render. Do not invent a power, default procedure,
service method or costs consequence. Reasons go in submissions.

## Affidavits and witness statements

- Use the forum's oath or affirmation wording and template. Introduce each person
  once by full name, short form and relationship.
- Distinguish personal knowledge, information and belief, recollection and
  inference as the form requires. Describe lived experience in lay terms unless
  admissible medical evidence supports a diagnosis.
- Do not convert disputed conduct, another document's allegation or another
  proceeding's submission into a fact. Annex only what is needed and permitted.

## Statutory declarations

A declaration is made under a declarations Act outside a proceeding; an
affidavit is filed in one; a witness statement is where a rule or direction
permits it. Do not use one where the forum requires another.

- The regime follows the requesting recipient and the law requiring the
  declaration, not the declarant's home state. Verify the Act, the prescribed
  wording, who may witness, and whether electronic or audio-visual witnessing is
  permitted. A declaration under the wrong Act, or witnessed by someone that Act
  does not authorise, can be worthless though true. If the regime is unclear,
  stop at an internal draft.
- Reproduce the prescribed declaration clause word for word; never paraphrase,
  move, split or add to it.
- Numbered first-person paragraphs, attachments marked as the regime requires,
  full witness block.
- No argument and no legal conclusions the declarant cannot know (that a
  contract was void, conduct unlawful or another person's intent). A gap is
  stated plainly or left out, never smoothed.
- False declaration is an offence for the declarant, and a drafter who knowingly
  procures one is exposed too. Never draft around a fact the declarant says is
  uncertain, never include a paragraph the declarant has not confirmed, never
  merge two people's knowledge, and escalate any conflict between instructions
  and a source document.

## Deeds and agreements

- Do not switch an instrument between deed and agreement in a rewrite. A deed
  says so, uses deed execution blocks and is dated as one; an instrument that
  calls itself a deed but executes like an agreement has a live defect.
- Recitals carry no operative effect: an obligation that must bind goes in an
  operative clause. One obligation per clause (actor, act, object, trigger).
  `must` for obligations, `may` for discretion. Periods state business or
  calendar days, whose calendar, and inclusivity. Money states currency, GST
  treatment and when payable.
- Boilerplate has effect (governing law, notices, variation, entire agreement,
  assignment, severance, counterparts and electronic execution, costs and GST).
  Identify what a general clause does before shortening or cutting it.
- Execution blocks match party type (individual, company, attorney, trustee,
  guarantor); verify the route, and never copy a block from a different party
  type. Check trustee capacity and power, and any guarantor advice or witnessing
  requirement.
- Reviewing: tabulate obligation, obliged party, trigger, deadline, consequence
  and clause before commenting. Report an ambiguity as an ambiguity; do not
  choose a reading or repair the other side's error without instruction.
- Signing, exchanging, dating, stamping and lodging are separately authorised.

## Correspondence

Draft as if it will be annexed. Courteous, firm, practical; answer the issue, not
the emotion in the last message.

- Request, answer or attachment list first. One topic per paragraph; one dated
  sentence plus anchor in place of repeated history. List only what is attached.
- Make no threat, concession, undertaking, offer, service admission or rights
  reservation unless the user instructed it and the forum context supports it. If
  a reply is likely to escalate, draft a short holding reply or suggest a
  non-email step.
- Match the recipient's register and thread. Do not carry a person's catchphrases
  from earlier letters.
- Before sending: right recipient, `Cc` and attachments; no protected address or
  unsafe contact detail; no unsupported accusation; no internal agent language or
  placeholders.

## Demands, pre-action letters and offers

A demand is a document the other side may put before a court.

- Verify before drafting: the recipient's legal identity (company or trustee
  name, entity number, personal or representative capacity), dated and anchored
  facts, the clause, provision or order relied on, any nearby limitation period,
  and any required pre-action step or prescribed statutory notice. A general
  demand cannot replace a prescribed notice, and a prescribed notice should not
  be diluted with extra demands.
- Structure: parties and matter, then the act or amount demanded and the dated
  deadline, then a short anchored fact sequence, the legal basis in a sentence or
  two, the consequence limited to steps the client is instructed and entitled to
  take, how to respond, and only the attachments needed to check the claim.
- Do not threaten a step the client cannot or is not instructed to take. Never
  threaten criminal, regulatory or immigration consequences for civil advantage.
  Do not misstate legal effect; debt-recovery statements to consumers and small
  business are regulated conduct. Take extra care where the recipient is
  self-represented, vulnerable or affected by a safety issue.
- Labels are used only on instruction, and the content and occasion decide the
  effect, not the label. `Without prejudice` protects a genuine settlement
  attempt, not a letter making no offer and not a threat.
  `Without prejudice save as to costs` (the Calderbank pattern) is for offers to be shown on costs;
  a formal offer under the forum's rules works only if the rule's form, content
  and timing are met. Non-admission wording does not cure an admission of fact
  elsewhere in the letter. A reservation of rights preserves only a right
  actually held.
- An offer states what is offered, what it settles, what each party must do, how
  long it stays open, and whether it includes costs and interest, including the
  release terms.
- Responding: decide the posture first (deny, admit in part, seek particulars,
  counter-offer, seek time); answer every demanded item; correct errors with
  anchors; do not admit a fact, debt, service or liability without instruction;
  do not adopt the sender's characterisation. Send a short holding reply naming a
  date rather than silence, which can carry consequences under a protocol,
  notice clause or statutory regime.

## Decision overviews

A non-filing page that helps decision-makers see resolved and open legal work.
Per issue, include only the fields the reader needs: position or options; status
(agreed, proposed, undecided, awaiting confirmation); legal basis at the source's
actual confidence; decision still reserved to the decision-makers; professional
confirmation still needed. Use neutral shared-position wording unless individual
attribution affects authority. Keep a limitation only where it changes reliance,
not as routine caveat in every row. Silence, inference or unconfirmed material is
never approval.
