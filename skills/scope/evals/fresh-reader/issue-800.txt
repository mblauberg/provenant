# Two specs carry history and rationale that ADR 0017 assigns elsewhere

Found by Gemini 3.8 Flash review lanes during the #798/#799 documentation pass, verified against the files. Both are larger than a copy-edit: they change what a document is for, so they are filed rather than applied.

Under [ADR 0017](docs/adr/0017-specifications-own-non-derivable-intent.md) a specification owns durable, non-derivable requirements. Under [ADR 0024] Git log and ADRs own change history. These two files hold material that neither rule assigns to them.

## 1. `docs/specs/harness/disclosure-refactor.md`

Reads as a migration ledger and interview record rather than a specification. It restates decisions D1 to D15, reproduces ADR 0014, carries July 2026 migration disposition tables, and specifies migration-only acceptance checks.

**Proposed:** rewrite as a normative specification of the invariants that survive the migration (ambient file rules, cross-skill referencing syntax, path constraints) and let Git history hold the decision record and disposition tables.

**Care needed:** the ambient-file and cross-skill-reference rules here are load-bearing, and `tests/test_install_harness.py` pins the ambient skill-name contract. Extract the invariants before deleting anything.

## 2. `docs/specs/harness/lifecycle.md`

Two sections carry rationale rather than requirements:

- Baseline evaluation scores from July 2026 (30/45 against 45/45) used to justify choosing `deliver`. That is a research finding.
- A "Known risks and controls" table, which is an architectural trade-off record.

**Proposed:** move the baseline narrative to the cited research document and the risks table to [ADR 0005](docs/adr/0005-lifecycle-kernel-extends-delivery.md), leaving the specification with its requirements.

## Not in scope

Neither change may weaken a requirement. If a sentence turns out to be the only statement of a rule, it stays in the specification and only its justification moves.
