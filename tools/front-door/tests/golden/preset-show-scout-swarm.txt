Preset scout-swarm

Pattern
  citations: ["Anthropic, how we built our multi-agent research system"]
  cost: width junior attempts + 1 senior synthesis
  level: recommended
  select_when: The scope of a task is unclear and a wide read-only survey is cheaper than guessing.
  summary: Junior read-only scouts map the problem in parallel; a senior synthesis reconciles their evidence.
  tags: ["research","read-only"]

Parameters
  external  integer  1  External scouts that research outward; 0 opts out and is recorded as an explicit parameter.
  read_roots  list  []  Extra read roots, for example another worktree.
  width  integer  4  Number of parallel scouts.

Stages
  {"id":"scouts","parallel":[{"count":"${width}","id":"scout","role":"scout.codebase"},{"count":"${external}","id":"external","role":"scout.external"}]}
  {"id":"synthesis","role":"plan.research"}

Exports
  {"node":"synthesis","outputs":["result","findings"]}
