Claude lanes accept --account LABEL from accounts.claude in telemetry.json.
Without it, cached quota excludes exhausted five-hour or seven-day windows and
favours expiring weekly reserve. When all eligible accounts lack usable cached quota,
labels sort alphabetically. If all configured accounts are exhausted, the lane refuses.
