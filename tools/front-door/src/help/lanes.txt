usage: provenant lanes [--json] [--all] [id…]
       provenant lanes --wait [--all] [--summary] [--timeout N] [id…]

Delegates to runtime/fabric/bin/fabric lanes ...
Default shows active lanes plus the 5 most recent terminal ones;
use --all to list all lanes.
`lanes --wait` waits for a lane to finish or need input. It exits 1 when a
lane it reports, or a lane it names, ended without success; 124 on timeout;
otherwise 0. --summary prints one bounded line per lane at the end.
