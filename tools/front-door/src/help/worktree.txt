usage: provenant worktree create BRANCH [--base REV]
       provenant worktree list
       provenant worktree remove NAME
       provenant worktree remove --merged --integration REF (--branch NAME… | --since OID)

Global options: --project PATH, --state-dir ABS, --json, --help.
Creates linked trees under .worktrees/ without dependency provisioning.
Removal refuses primary, dirty, live or unknown writers. Merged pruning is scoped.
