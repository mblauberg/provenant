# Native orchestration and discovery surfaces

One accountable chair owns coordination. Native execution stays within its assigned authority and does not become a second chair or a parallel owner of the same work. Discovery should expose tools supported by the principal and active capability, not unsupported stubs.

External observations alone do not prove completion or authority. [ADR 0039](../adr/0039-graph-of-templates.md) records the durable observation and action sequence; [routing policy](../specs/routing.md#policy-is-overridable-data) owns route selection.
