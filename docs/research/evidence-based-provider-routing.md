# Evidence-based provider routing

The evaluated unit is the deployed route, including its host, provider contract, model, execution settings and harness. Requested, admitted and observed identity are separate evidence; missing observations remain unknown. Provider benchmarks and price are priors, not proof of a local outcome.

The [routing spec](../specs/routing.md#policy-is-overridable-data) owns current policy. [ADR 0033](../adr/0033-weighted-route-pools-are-the-routing-unit.md) records the route selection decision and proposed admission boundary.
