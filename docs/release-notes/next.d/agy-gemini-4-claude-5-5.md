## Additions
### Accounts, quota and routing

- Bundled `agy` catalogue and pool support for the Gemini 4 family (`agy/gemini-4-hydrogen` in `junior`, `agy/gemini-4-neon` in `senior` and `principal`, `agy/gemini-4-argon` in `principal`) and Claude 5.5 (`agy/claude-sonnet-5-5` in `senior`, `writing` and `design`; `agy/claude-opus-5-5` in `principal` and `design`), with lower default pool weights (`weight = 1` on `agy` and `cursor` members against the standard bundled weight `2`) and efforts for usage pacing, family aliases (`agy/hydrogen`, `agy/neon`, `agy/argon`, `agy/flash`, `agy/sonnet`, `agy/opus`), and per-account `superseded_by` filtering so accounts offering Gemini 4 or Claude 5.5 omit `agy/gemini-3.8-flash` and Claude 4.6 from automatic pools while accounts on older model sets retain them.
- `agy` cached offered-model probes now invalidate when the account keychain stamp changes, and the `HOME` login falls back to canonical models from `model-discovery/agy.json` when no identity probe record exists yet. Bare `claude-sonnet-5-5` and `claude-opus-5-5` model selectors now require an explicit provider prefix (`claude/...` or `agy/...`) because both providers offer them.
