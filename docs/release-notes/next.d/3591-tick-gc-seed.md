## Fixes
### Lanes and settlement
- Retry an interrupted scheduled collection within minutes instead of waiting a day, and seed each project on its own. The daily collection deadline used to advance when the worker was launched, so a worker that was killed or failed cost a full day, and every tick then reported the stale failure as new. The deadline now advances only when a collection completes; a failed or interrupted one retries after 15 minutes, then 1 hour, 4 hours and 16 hours, capped at the daily cadence, a sweep that only stopped at its budget counts as progress rather than a failure, and tick reports the old failure as `gc=retry_pending` with its age and next retry. Seeding no longer stops at the first project whose verify base cannot be resolved: that project is recorded as skipped with a typed reason and a repair step, every other project is seeded, and tick reports counts such as `seed=ok:1 skipped:1`.
