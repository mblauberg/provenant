## Behaviour changes
### Pipelines and graphs

- The `scope`, `tracker` and `setup-repo` skills are rewritten. `scope` absorbs `grill-me` and `prototype` and ends in a one-screen approval digest. `tracker` defines Epic, Feature, Task and Bug, drops Decision issues and absorbs `work-map`. `setup-repo` ships Epic, Feature, Task and Bug issue forms and configures preset rules and the autonomy level (#2350).
