Provenant 2.9.9 adds a durable delivery queue that carries tracker items through CI, review and repair on each `tick`, and makes long-running lanes easier to watch and steer: a provider that goes quiet is reported as `stalled`, and `provenant steer` queues its text for the next turn unless you pass `--now`. Chair hooks now keep to one configurable time budget and a project-scoped digest, and the one breaking change renames the project `capabilities` key to `grants`, which `provenant install` migrates.
