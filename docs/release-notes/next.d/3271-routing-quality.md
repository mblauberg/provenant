## Behaviour changes
### Accounts, quota and routing

- Senior and principal routes exclude models below their declared capability floor. Override or disable the policy in routing config; when no member qualifies, the declared fallback warns and relaxes it. Receipts name the policy, source and excluded models (#3271).
- Eligible members retain capability preferences, reset-adjusted account load, outcome priors and exploration. Pacing reserves, uncertain partial headroom and Go allowance preferences warn and lower ranking (#3207).
