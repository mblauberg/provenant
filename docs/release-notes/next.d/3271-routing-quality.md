## Behaviour changes
### Accounts, quota and routing

- Senior and review routes prefer stronger models while keeping every eligible model available. Receipts show capability preferences, reset-adjusted account load, outcome priors and exploration; users can override the preferences or disable outcome learning (#3271, #3207).
- Pacing reserves, uncertain partial headroom and Go allowance preferences warn and lower ranking without blocking a ready route. Go and Zen each keep one account row across their models, with separate quota and pacing (#3256).
