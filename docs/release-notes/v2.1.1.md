# Provenant v2.1.1

Released 18 November 2026.

This patch keeps a lane's final answer when its provider output contains credential-shaped or malformed lines, and fixes review subjects and monthly pacing.

## Behaviour changes

- Agent guidance: small changes land without review. When review runs, the author is never its reviewer. `implement` starts a junior writer by default and `--route` picks another tier. Dated model remarks and fixed word and note caps are removed from the skills.

## Fixes

- Preserve raw local provider frames so credential-shaped tool output cannot truncate the settled final answer. Malformed replay reports an evidence defect with its line number; published results and output remain redacted. (#3395)
- Pin review lanes to a PR with `run review-panel --pr N` or to explicit subject and base parameters. Host diffs use the merge base, including branches that merge main; launches without a subject warn about the chosen range and reason. Retrying an admitted nested review replays its original receipt and pins even while the parent is active or the forge is unavailable. (#3465)
- Retain local-login monthly launch debt through quota ticks without retaining provider identity or readiness, drop removed registered accounts, preserve debt through delayed partial reports, pace local logins from sourced cycle estimates, admit busy-ledger launches with deferred reservations, and show monthly settings and budgets in route lists. (#3424)
