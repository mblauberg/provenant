# Provenant v1.4.2

Released 30 October 2026.

Provenant makes no stability promise; see [stability](../release-notes/README.md#stability).

## Additions

- `natural-writing` defaults to a plain, mildly casual voice and is restructured around a new voice reference (#2379).

## Fixes

- `reconcile` recovers writer lanes left settling behind reservations from older releases, preserving committed work and recovery evidence. Blocked settlement reports its reason, and repeated lane listings stop restarting workers after three failed recovery attempts; explicit reconciliation permits another attempt (#2374).
- Preset graph nodes that read bundled skills launch again on installed builds. Lanes may now read inside an installed release directory, and a graph that refuses a node's access reports the reason instead of `invalid input` (#2380).
