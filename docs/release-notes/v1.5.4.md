# Provenant v1.5.4

Released 3 November 2026.

This patch release fixes the menu bar app, which crashed at start-up in 1.5.3, and a console crash when resizing the window or switching sections. Routing now skips routes known to be unavailable, and review councils resolve their default subject in the bound worktree.

## Behaviour changes

- Routing reads persisted route readiness: a route whose account, model or pool is known to be unavailable (signed out, missing, or refused by the provider) is skipped and selection moves to another account or pool member. Unknown readiness never excludes a route. `route list`, `route resolve` and peer health show the readiness verdict and its reason (#2701, #2494).

## Fixes

- A review council bound to a worktree resolves its default `HEAD` subject in that worktree rather than the checkout that launched it, and warns when a node's commit differs from the bound worktree's `HEAD` (#2488).
- A review council's synthesis findings block is no longer rejected above 8 KiB; it stays bounded by the entry and field limits (#2700).
- The menu bar app launches from an installed copy again; since 1.5.3 it crashed at start-up and never showed its status item (#2725).
- The console no longer quits while resizing the window, toggling the inspector or switching sections; the Inbox list now has a fixed width (#2726).
