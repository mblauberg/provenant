# Provenant v1.6.9

Released 6 November 2026.

This release fixes problems found by real launches of 1.6.8: lanes now say when they are waiting on disk, keep the reason they were refused, and move to the next route member after a quota stop even when work has started. Read-only lanes also no longer wait on the disk floor.

## Behaviour changes

- Lanes without write authority below `gc.free_floor` warn once and proceed. Lanes with worktree, declared root, tool or project write authority continue to wait for enough disk space.

## Fixes

- Lanes on macOS can read `/dev/zero`, allowing commands such as `head -c N /dev/zero`.
- `provenant lanes` names disk waits with available space, `gc.free_floor` and its source instead of showing "preparing the lane".
- Lane listings omit bulky write-grant evidence so ordinary pages fit the JSON response limit. Use `lanes --id ID` to inspect the full grants.
- Named-route lanes retry another member after quota stops even when work has started. Writers retain their worktree and continue from existing files and commits, within the configured retry allowance.
- Launch preparation refusals retain a typed cause and safe guidance, including exact-model requests with no skipped routes. Writer disk refusals name disk pressure and how to restore space; unrelated rejections keep their generic diagnostic.
- Human `status` and `lanes` rows keep refusal and disk-wait labels concise. Structured reasons in listings, launch replies and output retain full remedies and lane-specific notes.
- Peer launch replies and explicit lane selections retain full refusal details, including the delivery Prepare step that failed.
- Launch preparation errors retain the full configuration remedy; compact reasons are reserved for human lane rows.
