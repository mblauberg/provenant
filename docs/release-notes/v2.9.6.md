# Provenant v2.9.6

Released 25 November 2026.

Provenant 2.9.6 is a reliability release. `reconcile --abandon` is no longer blocked by processes that vanish during the occupancy census or by a missing retained fence, and `stop` now settles an attempt stuck in Settling. Pre-lease writer reservations that never produced a holder are recovered by `reconcile` and GC, and `output` leads with settlement, verdict and findings.

## Fixes

- `output` now leads with settlement, verdict, findings and verify and keeps lane prose behind `--full`, so a compact read no longer embeds a head-and-tail of the result text. `--tail N` still bounds bytes and `--tail-lines N` bounds lines, both clamped consistently for local, peer, composite and event reads; fields that do not apply are omitted instead of printed as unavailable. Human output no longer carries ANSI escapes when stdout is not a TTY, and `--full` uses the parsed global flag rather than a raw argv scan. (#3807)
- `provenant stop` on a graph whose attempt is stuck in Settling with its provider gone now settles the attempt cancelled and keeps the captured evidence, instead of leaving it settling indefinitely. Prelaunch cancellation and deadline stops keep distinct evidence. (#3948, #3844)
- `provenant reconcile --abandon` no longer refuses a held writer attempt whose retained fence is missing with `INVALID`/`retained writer fence missing`. Abandon completes the attempt, leaves its worktree and uncommitted files untouched, and warns (`ABANDON_RETAINED_FENCE_MISSING`) with the custody slot that was to hold the fence. (#3960)
- A pre-lease writer reservation whose launch never produced a holder is now discovered and recovered by `provenant reconcile` and applied GC, including when no attempt row exists; uncertain custody remains fenced and attempt evidence is retained. It is no longer reported as unresolvable legacy evidence asking for a previous Provenant version. (#3949, #3844)
- The occupancy census excludes the calling Provenant process and its children, and a process that exits between listing and descriptor reading (including on Linux, where the executable lookup fails first) counts as absent instead of an unknown occupant, so `reconcile --abandon` is no longer blocked with `occupancy incomplete: descriptors unavailable`. A real permission failure on a live process still yields Unknown. (#3961)
- Lane scratch lives outside the attempt record directory, so scratch files no longer mix with settlement evidence. (#3951)
- GC sweeps when free space drops below a configurable floor, waits on a sweep already held instead of failing, and reports what it could not collect. (#3952)
