# Provenant v2.0.4

Released 17 November 2026.

This release makes lanes settle, stop and steer reliably without the chair, and accepts handoffs that providers write in `json` fences. Lane stop and guardian records changed format, so let running lanes settle or stop them before upgrading.

## Breaking changes

### Lanes and settlement

- Stop requests now live in `stop-intent.json` and guardian ownership in `guardian-lock.json`. A wall-deadline, budget or steer stop that an earlier release recorded for a running lane is ignored after the upgrade, and such lanes get no automatic guardian-loss recovery. Let running lanes settle, or stop them, before upgrading. (#3336, #3335)
- A steer in flight across the upgrade may refuse once, because the session identity digest changed; steer again after the upgrade. (#3359)

## Behaviour changes

### Lanes and settlement

- When a steered provider session is unavailable, settlement warns with the setting source and starts a fresh continuation carrying the accepted brief, prior handoff and queued steer text; set domains.fabric.steer.fallback = "refuse" to disable the fallback. (#3336)
### CLI and install

- `status` measures graph age from creation; `wait` names its all, any or fail-fast mode; target errors outside a project explain how to select one. (#3337)

### Fabric

- Custody-change warnings render holder IDs in their hexadecimal display form. (#3337)
### Lanes and settlement

- A writer lane's verification paragraph follows `lanes.verification` (`check` by default, or `targeted` or `full`). A brief line `Verification scope: …` overrides that knob and the preamble names source `brief`. (#3340)
### Accounts, quota and routing

- Unknown provider errors whose type and status are absent or unknown are classified as `unclassified`, not `usage_limited`: they preserve the provider message, retry once without marking the account exhausted, and fall back across eligible routes and providers at the same tier before stopping at the chair. (#3342)
- Readiness output distinguishes unprobed accounts from probed accounts where the provider returned no figures, and suppresses the `provenant quota tick` probe hint once an account has been probed. (#3342)
- Quota probe failures rank their account below ready or untested accounts during their validity window, and warn on launch when only failed-probe accounts remain unless refused by the `PROBE_FAILURE` launch policy knob. (#3287)
- OpenCode local-ledger estimates and auth reprobing allow unverified accounts to prepare without spurious exhaustion markings or swapping pinned accounts. (#3293)
### Lanes and settlement

- Lane cargo builds share one host cap, `domains.fabric.lanes.max_concurrent_builds`. Unset, the count comes from cores and memory. A busy cap waits a bounded time, then warns and continues. Set the value to `0` to disable it. (#3343)
### Lanes and settlement

- Review panels now settle with partial coverage when a lens is unavailable and name missing lenses in the result. Protected path floors still require their configured reviewer count and named lenses. (#3345)
### Lanes and settlement

- Lane instructions treat a write scope as the expected files, not a fence. A writer whose fix needs another file edits it minimally and lists it in its handoff instead of stopping as blocked; only a strict `WRITE_SCOPE` lane policy refuses those paths at settlement, #3354.
### Pipelines and graphs

- Review panels are smaller by default: standard runs three lenses and deep (integration heads and boundary paths) runs four, two junior and two senior, with `reliability` now third in the lens order. Boundary floors still add their lenses. Restore larger panels under `domains.routing.review.shapes`.

## Additions

### Pipelines and graphs

- Graph nodes can wait for predecessors to settle even if those predecessors fail. (#3345)

## Fixes

### Lanes and settlement

- Wall deadlines and explicit cancellation preserve commits already made on the lane branch and report the head, remaining dirty paths, and push outcome. They leave dirty files uncommitted; token-budget stops keep their existing safe snapshot commit. (#3281)
### Git and safety

- `provenant gc` collects settled worktrees backed by retained refs while preserving their local branches, and retires orphaned pruning receipts with explicit custody uncertainty instead of retrying forever. (#3296)
### Git and safety

- `provenant worktree remove` names the branch and missing landing proof when removal is unproven, with an explicit discard command for intentional removal. (#3297)
### Chair, inbox and console

- Graph ask polling treats a missing inbox request as pending until deadline default applies, rather than reporting stored state corruption. Corrupt requests continue to report state corruption detail. (#3301)
### Fabric

- Standardise child lane addition on the `prompt` wire field, matching compiled graph node keys and retiring stray `brief` spellings. (#3303)
### Lanes and settlement

- A lane whose writer rebased onto another branch now publishes. Publication accepts a tip that descends from the launch base, the origin default branch, the ref the lane launched against or any remote-tracking branch, records the ancestor in the reflog and `publication-ancestor.json`, and warns. A tip that descends from none of them settles `failed` with `LANE_TIP_UNROOTED` instead of staying `running`. (#3334)
- A handoff block repeated back to back is accepted with a warning: identical copies count once and differing copies settle on the last. (#3341)
### Lanes and settlement

- Guardians retain a dedicated kernel lock through terminal settlement, so
  recovery can prove same-boot guardian loss alongside free writer custody and
  a clear worktree census. Automatic settlement still requires Run driver
  integration. (#3335)
### Lanes and settlement

- Tick settles a lost guardian on the same boot when its original locks and an empty worktree census prove the attempt gone. Finished results remain available while descendant cleanup retains custody. (#3335)
### Lanes and settlement

- Steering uses a separate stop intent, so a steer is never mistaken for cancellation. While ownership or placement is being confirmed, the graph retains the instructions and retries with a warning. (#3336)
### Lanes and settlement

- `provenant land --json` refusals from GitHub's merge endpoint keep their typed leaf cause in `error.cause` (`HEAD_MOVED` for a 409, `MERGE_NOT_ALLOWED` for a 405) with a non-empty `error.repair`, under the unified `LAND_REFUSED` code, #3346.
### CLI and install

- Unknown graph templates and flavours name the requested template and list known choices. (#3350)
### Lanes and settlement

- Session hook context in lanes now directs progress to the lane handoff instead of suggesting a chair checkpoint save. (#3355)
